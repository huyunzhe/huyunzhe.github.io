VPFlow: Value-Predictive Planning with Imperfect World Models for Offline Goal Reaching
Yunzhe Hu, Dong Xu
arXiv preprint, 2026

A value-predictive planner for offline goal reaching: world models are trained to predict goal-reaching values rather than latent states, while noise-space planning enables policy improvement with imagined advantage without OOD queries. It challenges the pervasive assumption of next-state prediction in world models, showing that value prediction is more useful for planning, whereas accurate state prediction is often unnecessary.

A principled, top-down Transformer design grounded in energy-based interpretation: symmetric attention, feedforward, and RMS normalization all emerge from iterative constrained energy minimization on the hypersphere, yielding a compact recurrent-depth model competitive across reasoning, classification, and masked modeling tasks.
